What is Recruitment?

                    
Recruitment is the end-to-end process of identifying, attracting, interviewing, selecting, hiring, and onboarding employees.

                    
The Recruitment Lifecycle

                    
    
                        
  • 1. Requirement Gathering: Understanding what the client or hiring manager needs.
    • 
                        
  • 2. Sourcing: Actively looking for candidates on platforms like LinkedIn, Job Boards, or Github.
    • 
                        
  • 3. Screening: Evaluating the candidate's skills, experience, and cultural fit over a call.
    • 
                        
  • 4. Submission: Presenting the candidate's profile to the client.
    • 
                        
  • 5. Interviewing: Coordinating rounds between the client and candidate.
    • 
                        
  • 6. Offer & Onboarding: Negotiating salary, extending the offer, and ensuring they join.
    • 
                    

                    

                        Key Takeaway: A recruiter is a matchmaker. You are aligning a candidate's career goals with a company's business needs.
                    

                


                
                

                    
A Recruiter's Typical Day

                    
Success in recruitment is all about routine, pipeline management, and follow-ups.

                    
Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)

                    
    
                        
  • Check Emails & Messages: Respond to candidate replies on LinkedIn and email.
    • 
                        
  • Plan the Day: Review which jobs are urgent ("Live Roles") and prioritize them.
    • 
                        
  • Sourcing (Heavy Focus): Run Boolean searches to find fresh candidates and send out InMails/Emails to build the top of your funnel.
    • 
                    

                    
Afternoon (1:00 PM - 4:00 PM)

                    
    
                        
  • Screening Calls: Speak with candidates who responded to your morning outreach.
    • 
                        
  • Submissions: Format resumes and write summaries to submit qualified candidates to Account Managers or Clients.
    • 
                        
  • Interview Coordination: Schedule upcoming interviews and send prep emails to candidates.
    • 
                    

                    
Evening (4:00 PM - 6:00 PM)

                    
    
                        
  • Follow-ups: Check in with candidates who had interviews that day. "How did it go?"
    • 
                        
  • Pipeline Prep: Save new searches and prepare the job board alerts for tomorrow morning.
    • 
                    

                


                
                

                    
Roles & Responsibilities

                    
As a recruiter, your job isn't just finding resumes. It's managing people and expectations.

                    
    
                        
  • Candidate Qualification: You must ensure the candidate has the mandatory skills. Never submit a candidate hoping the client won't notice a missing skill.
    • 
                        
  • Rate Negotiation: Discussing salary expectations early. E.g., W2 vs C2C vs 1099.
    • 
                        
  • Relationship Building: Treat candidates with respect. A candidate who isn't a fit today might be perfect for a role next month.
    • 
                        
  • Market Intelligence: Educating the client/account manager if a requirement is unrealistic (e.g., asking for 10 years of experience in a technology that has only existed for 3 years).
    • 
                        
  • Ghosting Prevention: Keep candidates warm. Communicate frequently, even if the feedback is a rejection.
    • 
                    

                


                
                

                    
Boolean Searching: The Recruiter's Superpower

                    
Boolean logic allows you to combine keywords and modifiers to find exactly what you're looking for on LinkedIn, Google, or job boards.

                    
                    
The Basic Operators

                    

                        AND - Narrows your search. Results must include BOTH words.

                        Example: Java AND Spring
                    

                    

                        OR - Broadens your search. Results must include AT LEAST ONE word.

                        Example: Developer OR Engineer OR Programmer
                    

                    

                        NOT (or -) - Excludes terms.

                        Example: Manager NOT Project
                    

                    

                        "" (Quotes) - Exact phrase match.

                        Example: "Software Engineer"
                    

                    

                        () (Parentheses) - Groups complex logic.

                        Example: (Java OR Python) AND (AWS OR Azure)
                    


                    
Advanced X-Ray Searching (Google)

                    
Use Google to search inside specific sites like LinkedIn or Github.

                    site:linkedin.com/in "Software Engineer" AND ("React" OR "Angular") AND "San Francisco"
                

                
                

                    
IT Ecosystem & Roles

                    
As an IT recruiter, you must understand the technology landscape and the differences between various tech roles. This cheat sheet will help you route candidates correctly and speak their language.

                    
                    

                        
                        
                        

                            
Functional vs Technical Roles

                            
    
                                
  • Functional Roles: Focus on business processes, requirements, and project delivery.
    Example: Gathering requirements from a bank to build a new loan system.
    Titles: Business Analyst, Product Manager, Scrum Master.
    • 
                                
  • Technical Roles: Focus on coding, system design, and implementation.
    Example: Writing the Java code that processes the loan application.
    Titles: Software Engineer, Cloud Architect, Data Engineer.
    • 
                            


                            
Common IT Job Titles

                            
    
                                
  • Frontend Developer: Builds the user interface (what the user sees and clicks on). E.g., React, Angular.
    • 
                                
  • Backend Developer: Builds the server logic, APIs, and connects to databases. E.g., Java, Node.js, Python.
    • 
                                
  • Full Stack Developer: Handles both frontend and backend development.
    • 
                                
  • DevOps Engineer: Automates code deployments, manages servers, and cloud infrastructure.
    • 
                                
  • Data Engineer: Builds data pipelines and structures databases for analytics.
    • 
                                
  • Data Scientist / ML Engineer: Builds predictive AI models using data.
    • 
                                
  • Quality Assurance (QA) / SDET: Tests software for bugs and writes automated test scripts.
    • 
                                
  • Project Manager: Manages a specific project's timeline, budget, and resources.
    • 
                                
  • Program Manager: Manages a portfolio of multiple related projects to achieve strategic goals.
    • 
                            


                            
Types of Architects

                            
    
                                
  • Solutions Architect: Designs the overall technical solution for a specific business problem or application.
    • 
                                
  • Enterprise Architect: Looks at the company's entire technology landscape and ensures systems align with business strategy.
    • 
                                
  • Cloud Architect: Specializes in designing and migrating systems to cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP).
    • 
                                
  • Data Architect: Designs how enterprise data is stored, managed, and consumed across the organization.
    • 
                            

                        


                        
                        

                            
Core IT Ecosystems

                            
    
                                
  • Java Ecosystem: Java (Core), Spring Boot, Hibernate, Maven/Gradle, JUnit.
    • 
                                
  • JavaScript (Frontend): HTML/CSS, React, Angular, Vue, Webpack.
    • 
                                
  • JavaScript (Backend): Node.js, Express, NestJS.
    • 
                                
  • Python Ecosystem: Python, Django, Flask, Pandas, NumPy.
    • 
                                
  • C# / .NET Ecosystem: C#, .NET Core, ASP.NET, Entity Framework.
    • 
                                
  • Cloud & DevOps: AWS, Azure, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, Terraform, Ansible.
    • 
                                
  • Databases: SQL (MySQL, PostgreSQL, Oracle), NoSQL (MongoDB, Cassandra, Redis).
    • 
                            


                            
AI & Machine Learning

                            
    
                                
  • Languages: Python (Primary), R, Julia, C++.
    • 
                                
  • Frameworks: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, Keras.
    • 
                                
  • Generative AI & LLMs: OpenAI (GPT-4), Anthropic (Claude), Meta (Llama), Google (Gemini), Hugging Face.
    • 
                                
  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation): Techniques for grounding AI models in custom enterprise data (using Vector Databases like Pinecone/Milvus, and LangChain/LlamaIndex).
    • 
                                
  • MCP (Model Context Protocol): Standardized architecture for connecting AI models securely to external data sources and tools.
    • 
                            


                            
Niche Technologies

                            
    
                                
  • ERP: SAP (ABAP, FICO, HANA), Oracle EBS, Salesforce (Apex, Lightning), Workday.

  • Cybersecurity: Penetration Testing, SOC Analysis, IAM (Identity & Access Management), SIEM (Splunk).
    • 
                                
  • RPA: UiPath, Automation Anywhere, Blue Prism.
    • 
                                
  • Blockchain / Web3: Solidity, Ethereum, Smart Contracts.

IT Hierarchy

                            
    
                                
  • IC Track: Junior → Mid-Level → Senior → Staff/Lead → Principal.

  • Mgmt Track: Engineering Manager → Director → VP of Engineering → CTO / CIO.

Global Recruitment Geographies

                    
As a global recruiter, you must master the legal, tax, visa, and compensation structures of the region you are recruiting for. Study these terms carefully before taking your regional assessment.

                    
                    
                    

                        
🇺🇸 USA (United States)

                        
                        
Visas & Immigration

                        
    
                            
  • H1-B: Non-immigrant visa for foreign workers in specialty occupations. Highly regulated with an annual cap/lottery.
    • 
                            
  • OPT/CPT (F1 Visa): Optional/Curricular Practical Training. Allows international students to work in the US temporarily.
    • 
                            
  • TN Visa: Special work visa for citizens of Canada and Mexico under the USMCA agreement.
    • 
                            
  • Green Card: Permanent residency, allowing the individual to live and work permanently in the US.
    • 
                        


                        
Employment & Contract Types

                        
    
                            
  • W2: Direct, full-time employment. Employer withholds income taxes and pays payroll taxes. Usually includes benefits (health, 401k).
    • 
                            
  • 1099: Independent contractor. No taxes withheld; the contractor is responsible for their own taxes and receives no benefits.
    • 
                            
  • C2C (Corp-to-Corp): The recruitment agency pays a candidate's LLC or umbrella corporation rather than paying the candidate directly.
    • 
                            
  • Contract-to-Hire (C2H): Candidate starts on a contract (often W2 through the agency) with the intent to convert to a full-time employee of the client after 3-6 months.
    • 
                        


                        
Laws, Compliance & Security

                        
    
                            
  • I-9 Compliance: A mandatory federal form to verify the identity and legal employment authorization of individuals hired in the US.
    • 
                            
  • Security Clearances: (Secret, Top Secret, TS/SCI) Required for government/defense roles. Candidates usually must be US Citizens.
    • 
                            
  • At-Will Employment: In most US states, employers can terminate an employee at any time without establishing "just cause."
    • 
                        


                        
Compensation & Minimum Wage

                        
    
                            
  • Minimum Wage: Varies heavily by state and city (e.g., federal is $7.25/hr, but states like California or cities like Seattle mandate $16-$19+/hr).
    • 
                            
  • Exempt vs Non-Exempt: Exempt employees are salaried and don't get overtime. Non-exempt are hourly and get 1.5x pay for overtime (over 40 hrs/week).
    • 
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
🇮🇳 India

                        
                        
Employment & Work Culture

                        
    
                            
  • Notice Period: Very long compared to the West. Typically 30 to 90 days.
    • 
                            
  • Notice Period Buyout: When a hiring company urgently needs a candidate, they may pay the candidate's current employer to relieve them before the 90 days are up.
    • 
                            
  • Bench Sales: Marketing IT consultants who are between projects (sitting "on the bench").
    • 
                        


                        
Compensation & Taxation

                        
    
                            
  • CTC (Cost to Company): The total cost the employer spends on the employee. Includes basic, allowances, PF, and variable bonuses.
    • 
                            
  • In-Hand / Take-Home Salary: The actual amount credited to the bank account after tax and PF deductions. Often significantly lower than CTC divided by 12.
    • 
                            
  • PF (Provident Fund): Mandatory retirement savings. 12% of basic salary is deducted from the employee, and the employer matches it.
    • 
                            
  • TDS (Tax Deducted at Source): Income tax deducted automatically by the employer from the salary.
    • 
                            
  • Variable Pay: A portion of the CTC that is tied to company or individual performance and paid out quarterly or annually.
    • 
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
🇬🇧 UK & Europe

                        
                        
Visas & Immigration

                        
    
                            
  • Right to Work / BRP (UK): Biometric Residence Permit. Employers face massive fines (up to £60k per worker) for failing to verify legal Right to Work.
    • 
                            
  • EU Blue Card: A prestigious work and residence permit for non-EU highly skilled workers to work across most EU member states.
    • 
                            
  • Skilled Worker Visa (UK): Replaced the Tier 2 visa post-Brexit for sponsoring foreign talent.
    • 
                        


                        
Contract Types & Laws

                        
    
                            
  • IR35 (UK): Strict tax legislation for contractors. "Inside IR35" means the contractor is taxed as an employee. "Outside IR35" means they operate as a genuine independent business.
    • 
                            
  • Umbrella Company: Often used by contractors who are "Inside IR35". The umbrella company acts as their employer for tax/payroll purposes.
    • 
                            
  • GDPR (Europe): General Data Protection Regulation. Strict data privacy laws. You cannot keep a candidate's CV on file indefinitely without consent.
    • 
                            
  • Notice Periods: Typically 1 to 3 months for permanent staff, sometimes up to 6 months for executives.
    • 
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
🌏 Asia Pacific (APAC)

                        
                        
Visas & Immigration

                        
    
                            
  • Subclass 482 / TSS (Aus): Temporary Skill Shortage visa. Allows employers to sponsor foreign workers.
    • 
                            
  • Working Holiday Maker (WHM): Young adults traveling and working temporarily in Australia/NZ.
    • 
                            
  • Employment Pass (EP) (Singapore): Main work visa for foreign professionals, managers, and executives earning above a qualifying salary.
    • 
                        


                        
Compensation & Employment

                        
    
                            
  • Superannuation (Aus): Mandatory retirement system. Employers must pay a minimum percentage (e.g., 11.5%) on top of the employee's base salary into their "Super" fund.
    • 
                            
  • PAYG (Aus): Pay As You Go. The Australian system for withholding income tax from employees.
    • 
                            
  • 13th Month Pay (Philippines/Others): A legally mandated bonus given to employees (usually in December) equal to one month's salary.
    • 
                            
  • Minimum Wages: Australia has one of the highest national minimum wages in the world (approx. $23+ AUD/hr).
    • 
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
🇦🇪 Middle East

                        
                        
Immigration & Sponsorship

                        
    
                            
  • Iqama (Saudi Arabia): The national residency permit issued to expatriates. Employers must sponsor this.
    • 
                            
  • NOC (No Objection Certificate): Required in some Gulf states from a current employer to allow an expat to transfer their visa to a new company.
    • 
                            
  • Sponsorship (Kafala): The employer acts as the expat's sponsor, holding their visa and residency rights.
    • 
                        


                        
Laws & Local Quotas

                        
    
                            
  • Emiratisation (UAE) / Saudization (Nitaqat): Strict government quotas requiring private companies to hire a certain percentage of local citizens rather than expats.
    • 
                            
  • Work Week: Historically Sunday-Thursday, but recently shifted to Monday-Friday in the UAE to align with global markets.
    • 
                        


                        
Compensation & Benefits

                        
    
                            
  • Tax-Free Salaries: Many Gulf countries (like the UAE) do not levy personal income tax on salaries.
    • 
                            
  • End of Service Gratuity: A statutory severance payout based on the final basic salary and length of service, given to expats when they leave.
    • 
                            
  • Expat Packages: Besides base salary, offers often include housing allowances, transport allowances, annual flight tickets home, and family medical insurance.
    • 
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
🏢 Universal Job & Work Types

                        
    
                            
  • On-Site: 100% working from the employer's physical office.
    • 
                            
  • Remote: 100% working from home or anywhere (subject to tax jurisdictions).
    • 
                            
  • Hybrid: A mix (e.g., 3 days in office, 2 days remote).
    • 
                            
  • Permanent / FTE: Full-Time Employee on the company's direct payroll.
    • 
                            
  • Contract / Freelance: Temporary engagement for a specific duration or project scope.
    •

                            🇮🇳 India — Labour Law & HR Policy Framework
                            

                                
Governed by multiple central and state Acts including Factories Act, Payment of Wages Act, Minimum Wages Act, PF Act, ESIC Act, Gratuity Act, Shops & Establishment Acts, and the new Labour Codes (2019–2020).

                                

                                    

                                        
💰 Minimum Wages

                                        
    
                                            
  • Set by each state; no single national minimum wage
    • 
                                            
  • Central sphere: ₹178–₹423/day depending on skill level (unskilled/semi-skilled/skilled/highly skilled)
    • 
                                            
  • IT hubs: Bengaluru ~₹12,000/month unskilled; varies widely
    • 
                                            
  • Review cycle: Typically bi-annual or annual
    • 
                                            
  • Code on Wages 2019 proposes a National Floor Wage
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📋 Onboarding Checklist

                                        
    
                                            
  • Offer Letter + Appointment Letter (signed copy)
    • 
                                            
  • Background Verification (BGV) — typically 7–21 days
    • 
                                            
  • Form 11 (PF Declaration) + PF UAN linkage
    • 
                                            
  • ESIC Form 1 (if applicable — CTC ≤ ₹21,000/month)
    • 
                                            
  • Aadhaar, PAN Card copies
    • 
                                            
  • Bank account details for salary credit
    • 
                                            
  • Educational & experience certificate submission
    • 
                                            
  • POSH policy acknowledgement
    • 
                                            
  • NDA / IP assignment agreement
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🏖️ Leave Policy

                                        
    
                                            
  • Earned Leave (EL): 15–21 days/year (accrued monthly)
    • 
                                            
  • Casual Leave (CL): 7–12 days/year
    • 
                                            
  • Sick Leave (SL): 7–12 days/year
    • 
                                            
  • Maternity Leave: 26 weeks (for ≤2 children); 12 weeks thereafter
    • 
                                            
  • Paternity Leave: Not mandated centrally; typically 5–15 days via company policy
    • 
                                            
  • National Public Holidays: 3 mandatory (Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti) + state holidays
    • 
                                            
  • Leave encashment allowed on exit (EL balance)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📊 Salary & Compensation Structure

                                        
    
                                            
  • CTC (Cost to Company) = Gross Salary + Employer PF + Gratuity + Benefits
    • 
                                            
  • Basic: Typically 40–50% of CTC
    • 
                                            
  • HRA: 40–50% of Basic (metro/non-metro)
    • 
                                            
  • PF: 12% employer + 12% employee on Basic (≤₹15,000 mandatory)
    • 
                                            
  • Gratuity: 4.81% of Basic after 5 years of service
    • 
                                            
  • Variable Pay / Performance Bonus: Usually 10–20% of CTC
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📈 Hikes & Increments

                                        
    
                                            
  • Annual appraisal cycle: April–June (most companies)
    • 
                                            
  • Average IT increment: 8–15% (FY2024)
    • 
                                            
  • High performers: 15–25%+
    • 
                                            
  • Lateral/counter-offer hikes: 20–40% typical
    • 
                                            
  • No legal mandate for annual increments
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🚪 Termination & Notice Period

                                        
    
                                            
  • Notice period: 30–90 days (industry standard); IT: 60–90 days
    • 
                                            
  • Garden leave / Notice buyout: Common in IT sector
    • 
                                            
  • Retrenchment: 1 month notice or pay in lieu + 15 days salary per year (100+ employees)
    • 
                                            
  • Wrongful termination recourse: Industrial Disputes Act
    • 
                                            
  • Full & Final Settlement (FnF): Due within 30–45 days of exit
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🔎 Hiring & Interviewing Rules

                                        
    
                                            
  • No legal prohibition on asking age/marital status (but best practice avoids it)
    • 
                                            
  • POSH Act: Anti-sexual harassment policy mandatory (10+ employees)
    • 
                                            
  • Differently-abled candidates: Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016
    • 
                                            
  • Contract staffing: Governed by Contract Labour (R&A) Act 1970
    • 
                                            
  • Principal Employer liability for contractors
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🧾 Tax & Payroll Compliance

                                        
    
                                            
  • TDS on Salary: Employer must deduct under Section 192
    • 
                                            
  • New Tax Regime (default FY25): Slabs 0/5/10/15/20/30%
    • 
                                            
  • Professional Tax: State-level, up to ₹2,500/year
    • 
                                            
  • ESIC: 0.75% employee + 3.25% employer (if gross ≤₹21,000)
    • 
                                            
  • PT returns, PF ECR, ESIC returns monthly
    • 
                                            
  • Form 16 (salary TDS certificate) issued by May 31 each year
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                

                            

                        


                        
                        

                            🇺🇸 USA — Federal & State Labour Compliance
                            

                                
Governed primarily by FLSA, ADA, Title VII, FMLA, EEOC regulations, and state-level employment standards which often exceed federal minimums (e.g., California, New York).

                                

                                    

                                        
💰 Minimum Wage

                                        
    
                                            
  • Federal: $7.25/hour (unchanged since 2009)
    • 
                                            
  • California: $16.00/hour (2024); fast food workers: $20
    • 
                                            
  • New York City: $16.00/hour
    • 
                                            
  • Washington State: $16.28/hour
    • 
                                            
  • Texas / Florida: $7.25/hour (federal rate applies)
    • 
                                            
  • Tipped employees: Federal $2.13/hour (employer must top up to $7.25)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📋 Onboarding Checklist

                                        
    
                                            
  • I-9 Form (Employment Eligibility Verification) — Day 1
    • 
                                            
  • W-4 (Employee Withholding Allowance Certificate)
    • 
                                            
  • E-Verify check (federal contractors mandatory; state-level varies)
    • 
                                            
  • State new-hire reporting (within 20 days)
    • 
                                            
  • Benefits enrollment (health, dental, 401k)
    • 
                                            
  • Employee Handbook acknowledgement
    • 
                                            
  • Non-Compete / NDA (enforceability varies by state; FTC rule 2024)
    • 
                                            
  • Background check (FCRA compliant) + Drug test (if applicable)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🏖️ Leave Policy

                                        
    
                                            
  • FMLA: 12 weeks unpaid leave/year for eligible employees (50+ employee companies; 12+ months tenure)
    • 
                                            
  • No federal mandate for paid vacation — at-will employer discretion
    • 
                                            
  • PTO: Average 10–15 days; many tech companies: unlimited PTO
    • 
                                            
  • California: Mandatory paid sick leave (5 days/year)
    • 
                                            
  • NY Paid Family Leave: Up to 12 weeks at 67% pay
    • 
                                            
  • Federal holidays: 11 (not mandatory for private employers)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📊 Compensation & Pay

                                        
    
                                            
  • Salary expressed as annual (e.g., $120,000/year) or hourly
    • 
                                            
  • W2: Direct employee; employer withholds taxes
    • 
                                            
  • 1099/C2C: Independent contractor; self-employment tax
    • 
                                            
  • FLSA Exempt vs Non-Exempt: Exempt employees ($684+/week) not entitled to overtime
    • 
                                            
  • Overtime: 1.5× regular rate for 40+ hours/week (non-exempt)
    • 
                                            
  • Pay Transparency laws: CA, CO, NY, WA require salary ranges in job postings
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📈 Hikes & Increments

                                        
    
                                            
  • No legal mandate for raises; merit-based
    • 
                                            
  • Average annual raise: 3–5% (2024 projections)
    • 
                                            
  • Tech sector: Promotion-based hikes often 10–25%
    • 
                                            
  • RSU vesting schedules common in lieu of cash raises
    • 
                                            
  • Cost-of-Living Adjustments (COLA) common in unionized sectors
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🚪 Termination

                                        
    
                                            
  • At-Will Employment: Employer or employee may terminate at any time for any legal reason
    • 
                                            
  • WARN Act: 60 days notice for mass layoffs (100+ employees)
    • 
                                            
  • Severance: No federal mandate; contractual/policy-based
    • 
                                            
  • Final paycheck: Same day (CA) to next pay cycle (varies by state)
    • 
                                            
  • COBRA: Allows continuation of health insurance for 18 months post-termination
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🔎 Hiring & EEO

                                        
    
                                            
  • Protected classes: Race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age (40+), disability, pregnancy
    • 
                                            
  • Ban-the-Box laws: Many states restrict criminal history questions (e.g., CA, NY)
    • 
                                            
  • Salary history ban: CA, NY, IL and others prohibit asking prior salary
    • 
                                            
  • ADA: Reasonable accommodation required for qualified candidates with disabilities
    • 
                                            
  • OFCCP compliance for federal contractors (affirmative action)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🧾 Tax & Payroll

                                        
    
                                            
  • Federal Income Tax: 10–37% (2024 brackets)
    • 
                                            
  • Social Security: 6.2% employee + 6.2% employer (up to $168,600 wage base)
    • 
                                            
  • Medicare: 1.45% employee + 1.45% employer (+ 0.9% additional for >$200k)
    • 
                                            
  • FUTA: 6% employer (on first $7,000); SUTA varies by state
    • 
                                            
  • 401(k): Employee up to $23,000/year; employer match varies
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                

                            

                        


                        
                        

                            🇸🇬 Singapore — MOM Employment Act Framework
                            

                                
Governed by the Employment Act (Cap. 91), Employment of Foreign Manpower Act, MOM guidelines, and the Fair Consideration Framework (FCF). Singapore has no minimum wage except for selected sectors (Progressive Wage Model).

                                

                                    

                                        
💰 Minimum Wage & PWM

                                        
    
                                            
  • No universal statutory minimum wage
    • 
                                            
  • Progressive Wage Model (PWM) mandatory in: Cleaning, Security, Landscape, F&B, Retail (from 2023)
    • 
                                            
  • PWM Cleaning (2024): $1,700–$2,430/month (entry to senior)
    • 
                                            
  • Local Qualifying Salary (LQS): $1,600/month (for S-Pass/EP quota purposes)
    • 
                                            
  • EP minimum fixed salary: $5,000/month (Financial Services: $5,500)
    • 
                                            
  • S-Pass: $3,150/month minimum
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📋 Onboarding Checklist

                                        
    
                                            
  • Employment Contract (written if 14+ days duration)
    • 
                                            
  • Key Employment Terms (KETs) document within 14 days
    • 
                                            
  • CPF registration and contribution setup (Singapore Citizens/PRs only)
    • 
                                            
  • MOM work pass lodgement (EP/S-Pass/WP for foreigners)
    • 
                                            
  • MediShield Life & CareShield Life awareness
    • 
                                            
  • Skills Development Levy (SDL) registration
    • 
                                            
  • Itemised payslips (mandatory monthly)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🏖️ Leave Entitlements

                                        
    
                                            
  • Annual Leave: 7 days (1st year) → 14 days (8th year+); pro-rated
    • 
                                            
  • Sick Leave: 14 days outpatient + 60 days hospitalisation/year (after 6 months)
    • 
                                            
  • Maternity Leave: 16 weeks (SC/PR child); 8 weeks (foreigner child)
    • 
                                            
  • Paternity Leave: 2 weeks (SC child)
    • 
                                            
  • Childcare Leave: 6 days/year (SC child) / 2 days (non-SC)
    • 
                                            
  • Public Holidays: 11 gazetted days
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📊 CPF & Compensation

                                        
    
                                            
  • CPF (Central Provident Fund): SG Citizens & PRs only
    • 
                                            
  • Employee contribution: 20% (age ≤55)
    • 
                                            
  • Employer contribution: 17% (age ≤55)
    • 
                                            
  • Accounts: OA (37%), SA (10%), MedSave (10%) of total CPF
    • 
                                            
  • No CPF for EP/S-Pass holders (foreigners)
    • 
                                            
  • Annual bonus: AWS (Annual Wage Supplement / 13th month) — customary but not mandatory
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🔎 Fair Consideration Framework

                                        
    
                                            
  • All companies must advertise on MyCareersFuture.sg for 14 days before applying for EP
    • 
                                            
  • Unfair practices: Cannot prioritise foreigners over equally qualified Singaporeans
    • 
                                            
  • MOM can investigate companies with skewed foreign/local hiring ratios
    • 
                                            
  • Job advertisements must not contain nationality/language preferences unless justified
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🚪 Termination

                                        
    
                                            
  • Notice period: Contractual (typically 1–3 months for PMEs)
    • 
                                            
  • Retrenchment benefit: Not legally mandated, but ≥2 years service: typically 2 weeks–1 month per year of service
    • 
                                            
  • MOM retrenchment notification required if 5+ employees retrenched in any 6-month period
    • 
                                            
  • Wrongful dismissal: Can file to Employment Claims Tribunal (ECT) within 1 month
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                

                            

                        


                        
                        

                            🇦🇪 UAE & Gulf Region — Labour Law Overview
                            

                                
UAE Federal Labour Law (Federal Law No. 33 of 2021) governs employment. Each Gulf state (KSA, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait) has its own labour law. No personal income tax in any GCC country.

                                

                                    

                                        
💰 Minimum Wage

                                        
    
                                            
  • UAE: No universal statutory minimum wage (Emiratis in private sector: AED 4,000–5,000 minimum support scheme)
    • 
                                            
  • KSA: No minimum wage for expats; Saudi nationals: SAR 4,000/month minimum
    • 
                                            
  • Qatar: QAR 1,000/month minimum wage (2021) + QAR 300 food + QAR 500 housing allowance
    • 
                                            
  • All GCC: No income tax on salaries
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📋 Onboarding (UAE)

                                        
    
                                            
  • UAE Residence Visa + Work Permit (Labour Card) — employer-sponsored
    • 
                                            
  • Emirates ID registration (within 30 days of visa stamping)
    • 
                                            
  • UAE Labour Contract registered with MOHRE
    • 
                                            
  • Medical fitness test (mandatory for visa)
    • 
                                            
  • UAE bank account opening
    • 
                                            
  • WPS (Wages Protection System) enrollment — mandatory for salary payment
    • 
                                            
  • DEWS / GPSSA enrollment (pension for GCC nationals in UAE)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🏖️ Leave Entitlements (UAE)

                                        
    
                                            
  • Annual Leave: 30 days/year (after 1 year service)
    • 
                                            
  • Sick Leave: 90 days/year: first 15 days — full pay, next 30 — half pay, next 45 — no pay
    • 
                                            
  • Maternity Leave: 60 days (45 full pay + 15 half pay)
    • 
                                            
  • Paternity Leave: 5 days
    • 
                                            
  • Hajj Leave: 30 days (unpaid, once per service)
    • 
                                            
  • Public Holidays: ~14 days (Islamic holidays are lunar-based)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📊 Gratuity & End of Service

                                        
    
                                            
  • Gratuity mandatory after 1+ year of service
    • 
                                            
  • Calculation: 21 days basic salary per year (first 5 years) + 30 days per year (thereafter)
    • 
                                            
  • Maximum gratuity: 2 years' total basic salary
    • 
                                            
  • DEWS (Dirhams End of Service Scheme): Optional alternative to gratuity (investment-based)
    • 
                                            
  • No employer/employee pension contributions for expats
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🔎 Hiring Rules

                                        
    
                                            
  • Emiratisation (Nationalization) targets: Private firms 50+ employees: 2% Emirati increase/year
    • 
                                            
  • Skilled categories: Compulsory 10% Emirati rate for certain roles
    • 
                                            
  • Kafala (Sponsorship) system: Employer sponsors expat's legal stay
    • 
                                            
  • Job offer letters must match the MOHRE-registered contract
    • 
                                            
  • Anti-discrimination: UAE bans discrimination by race, religion, gender
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🚪 Termination (UAE)

                                        
    
                                            
  • Notice period: Minimum 30 days (up to 90 days per contract)
    • 
                                            
  • Arbitrary dismissal: Employee can claim up to 3 months' compensation
    • 
                                            
  • Visa cancellation: Employer must cancel within 30 days of termination
    • 
                                            
  • Labour ban (old system): Now largely abolished post-2021 law
    • 
                                            
  • Disputes: MOHRE mediation → Labour Court
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                

                            

                        


                        
                        

                            🇦🇺 Australia — Fair Work Act & National Employment Standards
                            

                                
Australia's employment framework is primarily governed by the Fair Work Act 2009, the National Employment Standards (NES), and Modern Awards/Enterprise Agreements. The Fair Work Commission (FWC) sets minimum wage annually.

                                

                                    

                                        
💰 Minimum Wage

                                        
    
                                            
  • National Minimum Wage: AUD $23.23/hour ($882.80/week) — from July 2024
    • 
                                            
  • Modern Award minimum rates vary by award and classification
    • 
                                            
  • Award-free employees covered by NMW
    • 
                                            
  • Casual loading: 25% above base rate
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📋 Onboarding Checklist

                                        
    
                                            
  • Fair Work Information Statement (mandatory Day 1)
    • 
                                            
  • TFN (Tax File Number) declaration
    • 
                                            
  • Superannuation fund choice form (14 days)
    • 
                                            
  • Right to work check (VEVO for visa holders)
    • 
                                            
  • State-based WorkCover/workers' compensation registration
    • 
                                            
  • Bank account for salary payment
    • 
                                            
  • WHS (Work Health & Safety) induction
    • 
                                            
  • Privacy Act acknowledgement + any relevant licences
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🏖️ NES Leave Entitlements

                                        
    
                                            
  • Annual Leave: 4 weeks/year (5 weeks for shift workers)
    • 
                                            
  • Personal/Carer's Leave: 10 days/year (paid)
    • 
                                            
  • Parental Leave: 12 months unpaid (+ 2 weeks government-paid scheme)
    • 
                                            
  • Community Service Leave: Unpaid (jury duty: employer must pay base rate)
    • 
                                            
  • Long Service Leave: After 7–10 years (state-based)
    • 
                                            
  • Public Holidays: 8–10 days (state-based)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📊 Superannuation

                                        
    
                                            
  • Mandatory employer contribution: 11% of ordinary earnings (from July 2023)
    • 
                                            
  • Rising to 11.5% (July 2024) → 12% (July 2025)
    • 
                                            
  • Paid quarterly at minimum (monthly from 2026)
    • 
                                            
  • Choice of fund: Employee can select their own super fund
    • 
                                            
  • Stapled fund: ATO assigns a super fund to new employees without a choice
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🚪 Termination & Redundancy

                                        
    
                                            
  • Notice period: 1–4 weeks depending on years of service
    • 
                                            
  • Unfair dismissal: Employees with 6+ months tenure (12 months small business) can claim unfair dismissal via FWC
    • 
                                            
  • Redundancy pay: 4–16 weeks depending on years of service (NES scale)
    • 
                                            
  • Genuine redundancy: Must consult and consider redeployment
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🧾 Tax & Payroll

                                        
    
                                            
  • PAYG withholding (employer withholds income tax)
    • 
                                            
  • Tax-free threshold: AUD $18,200/year
    • 
                                            
  • Marginal rates: 19%, 32.5%, 37%, 45%
    • 
                                            
  • Medicare Levy: 2% of taxable income
    • 
                                            
  • Single Touch Payroll (STP): Real-time ATO reporting mandatory
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                

                            

                        


                        
                        

                            🇬🇧 United Kingdom — Employment Law & Workers' Rights
                            

                                
UK employment is governed by the Employment Rights Act 1996, Working Time Regulations 1998, Equality Act 2010, National Minimum Wage Act 1998, and post-Brexit immigration rules. GDPR (UK-GDPR) applies to all employee data.

                                

                                    

                                        
💰 National Living / Minimum Wage

                                        
    
                                            
  • National Living Wage (21+): £11.44/hour (April 2024)
    • 
                                            
  • 18–20 years: £8.60/hour
    • 
                                            
  • 16–17 years / Apprentice: £6.40/hour
    •

                            🌏 Philippines & Southeast Asia — DOLE & Regional Labour Laws
                            

                                
The Philippines is governed by the Labor Code of the Philippines (PD 442) and DOLE regulations. Other SEA countries (Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam) each have distinct labour codes. This section focuses on the Philippines with notes on key SEA variations.

                                

                                    

                                        
💰 Minimum Wage (Philippines)

📋 Onboarding (Philippines)

  • PhilHealth registration (HMO coverage)
    • 
                                            
  • Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF) registration
    • 
                                            
  • DOLE employment report (for 5+ new hires)
    • 
                                            
  • Company ID + pre-employment medical
    • 
                                            
  • NBI Clearance / Police Clearance (BGV)
    • 
                                            
  • Contract of Employment (regular vs probationary)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🏖️ Leave Entitlements (Philippines)

  • Maternity Leave: 105 days paid (+ 30 days unpaid extension); 120 days solo parent

    • 
                                            
  • Parental Leave (solo parent): 7 days/year

    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📊 Statutory Contributions (Philippines)

                                        
    
                                            
  • SSS: Employee 4.5% + Employer 9.5% (salary credit basis)
    • 
                                            
  • PhilHealth: 5% total — 2.5% employee + 2.5% employer (2024)
    • 
                                            
  • Pag-IBIG (HDMF): PHP 100/month employee + PHP 100 employer (or more)
    •

🔎 Employment Classifications

                            🇪🇺 Europe — EU Directives & Key Country Labour Laws
                            

                                
The EU sets minimum standards via Directives (Working Time Directive, Equal Treatment, GDPR, Transparent & Predictable Working Conditions Directive 2022). Each Member State implements these through national law — which often far exceeds EU minimums. Key staffing markets: 🇩🇪 Germany, 🇫🇷 France, 🇳🇱 Netherlands, 🇮🇪 Ireland, 🇵🇱 Poland.

                                

                                    

                                        
💰 Minimum Wages (2024)

                                        
    
                                            
  • Germany: €12.41/hour (statutory Mindestlohn)
    • 
                                            
  • France: €11.65/hour (SMIC — Salaire Minimum Interprofessionnel de Croissance)
    • 
                                            
  • Netherlands: €13.27/hour (for 21+)
    • 
                                            
  • Ireland: €12.70/hour (Living Wage target from Jan 2024)
    • 
                                            
  • Poland: PLN 4,242/month (Jan 2024); PLN 4,300 from Jul 2024
    • 
                                            
  • Denmark / Sweden / Norway: No statutory minimum — set by collective bargaining agreements (CBA)
    • 
                                            
  • EU Minimum Wage Directive (2022): Targets 60% of median wage as benchmark
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📋 Onboarding Checklist (EU-wide)

                                        
    
                                            
  • Written employment statement within 7 days of start (EU Directive 2019/1152)
    • 
                                            
  • GDPR-compliant privacy notice for employee data processing
    • 
                                            
  • Social security registration (each country's system)
    • 
                                            
  • Tax registration (e.g., Steuer-ID in Germany, PPS in Ireland, BSN in Netherlands)
    • 
                                            
  • Works Council notification (if applicable — Germany, France mandate this for workforce changes)
    • 
                                            
  • Right to work verification (EEA nationals: free movement; non-EEA: national work permit)
    • 
                                            
  • Collective Agreement (Tarifvertrag/Convention Collective) disclosure where applicable
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🏖️ Leave Entitlements

                                        
    
                                            
  • EU Minimum Annual Leave: 4 weeks (20 days) — Working Time Directive
    • 
                                            
  • Germany: 20 days statutory minimum; most CBAs grant 25–30 days
    • 
                                            
  • France: 25 days paid AL (5 weeks); RTT days for 35-hour week compliance
    • 
                                            
  • Netherlands: 20 days minimum + 8% holiday allowance (vakantiegeld) paid annually
    • 
                                            
  • Germany Maternity: 14 weeks (6 weeks before + 8 after birth); Elterngeld (parental allowance) up to 14 months
    • 
                                            
  • France Maternity: 16 weeks; Paternity: 25 days
    • 
                                            
  • Parental Leave: EU Directive: 4 months per parent (2 months non-transferable)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📊 Social Security & Payroll

                                        
    
                                            
  • Germany (SV contributions): Pension 18.6% (split 50/50), Health 14.6% + add'l, Unemployment 2.6%, Care 3.4% — employer pays ~20% on top of gross salary
    • 
                                            
  • France: Employer charges ~42–45% on top of gross salary (cotisations sociales)
    • 
                                            
  • Netherlands: Employee NI contributions ~27%; employer ~18% — complex sliding scale
    • 
                                            
  • Ireland: PRSI: Employee 4% + Employer 11.15% on earnings above €352/week; USC 0.5–8%
    • 
                                            
  • EU: No pan-EU payroll — each country runs independent social security
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🔎 Hiring & Works Councils

                                        
    
                                            
  • Germany: Betriebsrat (Works Council) has co-determination rights on hiring, dismissals, working hours in companies with 5+ employees
    • 
                                            
  • France: Comité Social et Économique (CSE) mandatory for 11+ employees
    • 
                                            
  • EU Equal Treatment Directive: No discrimination by gender, race, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation
    • 
                                            
  • Posted Workers Directive: Workers posted from one EU state to another get host country's minimum conditions
    • 
                                            
  • Germany: Temporary staffing (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung) capped at 18 months per placement
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🚪 Termination

                                        
    
                                            
  • Germany: Kündigungsschutzgesetz (KSchG) — after 6 months, dismissal requires social justification; works council must be consulted; notice 2 weeks–7 months by tenure
    • 
                                            
  • France: CDD (fixed-term) / CDI (permanent); CDI dismissal requires justified grounds; severance: 1/4 month/year salary (up to 10 yrs), 1/3 thereafter
    • 
                                            
  • Netherlands: UWV (government) or court approval required for most dismissals; transition payment: 1/3 month/year of service
    • 
                                            
  • Ireland: Unfair dismissal rights after 12 months; notice 1–8 weeks by tenure
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🛡️ GDPR & Data Protection

                                        
    
                                            
  • GDPR (EU 2016/679) applies to all employee data across EU/EEA
    • 
                                            
  • Lawful basis for processing HR data: Contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest
    • 
                                            
  • Data retention: Must not keep employee data longer than necessary
    • 
                                            
  • Subject Access Requests (SAR): Employees can request all data held about them (30-day response window)
    • 
                                            
  • Cross-border transfers: Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) required for data outside EEA
    • 
                                            
  • DPO (Data Protection Officer): Mandatory for large-scale HR data processing
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📈 Hikes & Increments

                                        
    
                                            
  • No statutory obligation for annual salary increases in most EU countries
    • 
                                            
  • CBA-covered employees: Sector-agreed pay scales often mandate annual step increases
    • 
                                            
  • Germany: Tariff wage increases vary by sector; IG Metall 2024: +5.5%
    • 
                                            
  • France: SMIC indexation triggers automatic raises for min-wage employees
    • 
                                            
  • EU Pay Transparency Directive (2026 enforcement): Requires pay range disclosure and reporting gender pay gap
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                

                            

                        


                        
                        

                            🇳🇿 New Zealand — Employment Relations Act & NES
                            

                                
Employment in New Zealand is governed by the Employment Relations Act 2000 (ERA), Holidays Act 2003, Minimum Wage Act 1983, and the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015. The government (MBIE) sets minimum standards and WorkSafe enforces H&S compliance.

                                

                                    

                                        
💰 Minimum Wage

                                        
    
                                            
  • Adult Minimum Wage: NZD $23.15/hour (Apr 2024)
    • 
                                            
  • Starting-Out / Training Wage: NZD $18.52/hour (80% of adult rate)
    • 
                                            
  • Reviewed annually by Cabinet (1 April each year)
    • 
                                            
  • Applies to all workers 16+ years; no tip credit system
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📋 Onboarding Checklist

                                        
    
                                            
  • Individual Employment Agreement (IEA) — must be signed before work commences
    • 
                                            
  • IRD number registration (Inland Revenue — tax)
    • 
                                            
  • KiwiSaver enrollment (automatic opt-in, employee can opt out)
    • 
                                            
  • ACC (Accident Compensation Corporation) employer levy registration
    • 
                                            
  • Right to work verification (NZ citizens/residents free; others require visa)
    • 
                                            
  • PAYE registration with Inland Revenue
    • 
                                            
  • Health & Safety induction (HSWA 2015)
    • 
                                            
  • Trial period clause (90-day trial for employers with <20 staff)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🏖️ Leave Entitlements (Holidays Act 2003)

                                        
    
                                            
  • Annual Holidays: 4 weeks/year after 12 months continuous employment
    • 
                                            
  • Sick Leave: 10 days/year (after 6 months); unused days carry over to max 20 days
    • 
                                            
  • Bereavement Leave: 3 days (immediate family); 1 day (others)
    • 
                                            
  • Parental Leave: 26 weeks primary carer; 1 week partner/paternity leave (paid by government)
    • 
                                            
  • Public Holidays: 12 days (including Waitangi Day, Anzac Day, local anniversary days)
    • 
                                            
  • Domestic Violence Leave: 10 days paid/year
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📊 KiwiSaver & ACC

                                        
    
                                            
  • KiwiSaver: Employee minimum 3% + Employer minimum 3% of gross earnings
    • 
                                            
  • Employee can opt for higher rates: 4%, 6%, 8%, 10%
    • 
                                            
  • Employer contributions taxed via ESCT (Employer Superannuation Contribution Tax)
    • 
                                            
  • ACC Earner Levy: Employee pays ~$1.60/NZD100 earnings
    • 
                                            
  • ACC Work Levy: Employer pays based on industry risk classification
    • 
                                            
  • ACC covers all work-related accidents (no-fault scheme — replaces tort liability)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🚪 Termination & Disputes

                                        
    
                                            
  • Employer must follow a fair and reasonable process for dismissal
    • 
                                            
  • 90-Day Trial Period: Employers with <20 employees can include this clause — dismissal during trial cannot be challenged for unjustified dismissal
    • 
                                            
  • Notice: Per employment agreement; typically 2–4 weeks
    • 
                                            
  • Redundancy: No statutory redundancy payment; contractual entitlement if agreed
    • 
                                            
  • Mediation: MBIE mediation is a free first step before Employment Relations Authority (ERA)
    • 
                                            
  • Unjustified dismissal penalty: Reinstatement (primary remedy) + compensation
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🧾 Tax (PAYE)

                                        
    
                                            
  • Income Tax (2024): Up to $14,000 = 10.5%; $14,001–$48,000 = 17.5%; $48,001–$70,000 = 30%; $70,001–$180,000 = 33%; $180,001+ = 39%
    • 
                                            
  • PAYE deducted by employer; employee files IR3 if additional income
    • 
                                            
  • GST (Goods and Services Tax): 15% — on benefits-in-kind, apply FBT rules
    • 
                                            
  • No payroll tax; no state income tax (national only)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🔎 Hiring Rules

                                        
    
                                            
  • Human Rights Act 1993: Protected grounds include race, sex, marital status, religious belief, political opinion, disability, age (16+), sexual orientation
    • 
                                            
  • Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV): Required to hire most migrants; employer must be INZ-accredited
    • 
                                            
  • Immigration NZ Skills Match Report: Employers must demonstrate genuine skills shortage for most work visas
    • 
                                            
  • Pay Equity: Pay Equity Act 2020 — female-dominated roles can claim pay equity with comparable male-dominated roles
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
📈 Hikes & Contractor Market

                                        
    
                                            
  • No statutory mandate for annual increments
    • 
                                            
  • Average market increases: 3–6% in 2024 (post-COVID normalisation)
    • 
                                            
  • IT contractor rates: NZD $90–$180/hour depending on specialisation and city (Auckland premium)
    • 
                                            
  • Contractors (self-employed): Must not be "sham contracting" — NZ law looks at actual work relationship
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                

                            

                        


                        
                        

                            🌏 APAC — Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong & Thailand
                            

                                
Asia Pacific (excl. India, Singapore, Australia, Philippines covered above) includes major hiring markets: Japan (Labour Standards Act), South Korea (Labor Standards Act), Hong Kong (Employment Ordinance Cap. 57), and Thailand (Labour Protection Act). Each has unique lifetime-employment cultural norms and compliance frameworks.

                                


                                    
                                    

                                        
🇯🇵 Japan — Minimum Wage & Pay

                                        
    
                                            
  • Regional Minimum Wage: ¥1,113/hour Tokyo (Oct 2024); national avg ~¥1,055
    • 
                                            
  • Salary expressed as annual (nenSHU 年収) or monthly (gesshu 月収)
    • 
                                            
  • 13th/14th month bonus (Bonasu 賞与): Typically July + December; avg 2–4 months' salary
    • 
                                            
  • Seniority-based pay (nenko-joretsu) still common, but merit pay growing
    • 
                                            
  • Overtime premium: 25–50% of hourly rate; 60-hour rule: 50%+
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🇯🇵 Japan — Leave & Social Security

                                        
    
                                            
  • Annual Leave: 10 days after 6 months (increases to 20 days by year 6.5)
    • 
                                            
  • Mandatory 5 days AL usage per year (since 2019)
    • 
                                            
  • Maternity: 14 weeks (6+8); Paternity: up to 4 weeks (paid via insurance)
    • 
                                            
  • Childcare Leave: Until child turns 2; paid ~67% via Employment Insurance
    • 
                                            
  • Social insurance: Kosei Nenkin (pension) ~9.15% each, Kenko Hoken (health) ~5% each
    • 
                                            
  • Hello Work: Government job placement system — employers must notify new hires within 5 days
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🇯🇵 Japan — Hiring & Termination

                                        
    
                                            
  • Labor Standards Act: Terminating a regular (seishain) employee is extremely difficult — courts often reinstate
    • 
                                            
  • Fixed-term employees: Max 5-year rule — can demand conversion to indefinite contract
    • 
                                            
  • Notice: 30 days advance or 30 days' pay in lieu
    • 
                                            
  • Probationary period: Up to 3 months (occasionally 6 months)
    • 
                                            
  • Work style reform (2019): Max 720 overtime hours/year (100/month in peak); mandatory
    • 
                                            
  • Foreign workers: "Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/Int'l Services" visa most common for IT/professional roles
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🇰🇷 South Korea — Minimum Wage & Pay

                                        
    
                                            
  • Minimum Wage: KRW 9,860/hour (2024); KRW 10,030 (proposed 2025)
    • 
                                            
  • Monthly salary standard: 40-hour week × 4.345 weeks
    • 
                                            
  • Severance Pay (Retirement Allowance): 1 month's avg wage per year of service — mandatory for all employees working 1+ year, 15+ hrs/week
    • 
                                            
  • Bonus culture: Variable by sector; Chuseok & Lunar New Year bonuses common
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🇰🇷 South Korea — Leave & Social Insurance

                                        
    
                                            
  • Annual Leave: 15 days (after 1 year); up to 25 days (by year 3+)
    • 
                                            
  • Maternity: 90 days (120 days for multiple births) — 60 days paid by employer, rest by NHIS
    • 
                                            
  • Paternity: 10 days paid
    • 
                                            
  • Parental Leave: 1 year per parent (until child is 8 years old)
    • 
                                            
  • 4 Social Insurances: NPS (pension) 4.5% each, NHIS (health) 3.545% each, EIS (employment) 0.9% each, WCIA (workers' comp) employer-only rate
    • 
                                            
  • 52-hour workweek cap (enforced for all companies since 2021)
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🇰🇷 South Korea — Hiring & Termination

                                        
    
                                            
  • Labor Standards Act: Dismissal must have "justifiable reason"; courts frequently rule in employee's favour
    • 
                                            
  • Notice: 30 days or 30 days' pay in lieu
    • 
                                            
  • Mass layoffs: 10% of workforce — must notify Ministry of Employment & Labor 60 days in advance
    • 
                                            
  • Employment Permit System (EPS): For non-professional foreign workers (manufacturing, agriculture)
    • 
                                            
  • E-7 (Special Occupation) visa: Common for professional/IT foreign hires
    • 
                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        
🇭🇰 Hong Kong — Employment Ordinance

                                        
    
                                            
  • Statutory Minimum Wage: HKD $40/hour (May 2023)
    • 
                                            
  • Annual Leave: 7 days (1st year) → 14 days (9th year+)
    • 
                                            
  • Sick Leave: 2 days/month (paid); accumulates to max 120 days
    • 
                                            
  • Maternity Leave: 14 weeks paid (extended from 10 weeks, 2020)
    • 
                                            
  • Paternity Leave: 5 days paid
    • 
                                            
  • MPF (Mandatory Provident Fund): Employee 5% + Employer 5% (on earnings $7,100–$30,000/month HKD)
    • 
                                            
  • Severance / Long Service Payment: After 2 years — 2/3 of last month's wages × years of service
