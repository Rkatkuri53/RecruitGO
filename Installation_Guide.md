# MyRecruiter: Complete Installation & Setup Guide

Welcome to **MyRecruiter**, your standalone AI-powered Recruitment Command Center! 

Because MyRecruiter runs **100% locally** and protects your candidate data by keeping it strictly on your personal computer, it requires a local AI engine to process resumes and analyze job descriptions. 

Please follow these 3 simple steps to get your Command Center fully operational:

---

## Step 1: Install the Core AI Engine
We use an industry-leading open-source AI engine called **Ollama** to power the intelligence of your dashboard.
1. Go to [ollama.com](https://ollama.com/)
2. Click **Download** and install it on your computer.
*(You do not need to do anything else with it—just ensure it is installed and running in the background).*

---

## Step 2: Install MyRecruiter
1. Locate the **`MyRecruiter Setup 1.0.0.exe`** file you received.
2. Double-click the file to install the application.
3. Once finished, the app will launch immediately, and a shortcut will be placed on your Desktop.

---

## Step 3: Download the AI Models via Dashboard
Now that the app is open, we need to download the specific "Brains" (AI Models) required for recruitment:
1. Look at the right side of your dashboard for the **🤖 AI Engine Health** card.
2. If you see a warning that the models are missing, simply click the purple **"⬇️ Download AI Models"** button.
3. **Wait a few minutes.** The app will seamlessly connect to the internet and download the massive AI models in the background. You can watch the real-time download progress at the bottom of the screen in your log terminal!
4. Once complete, the Health Card will turn green: `✅ AI Engine is fully operational!`.

---

## Step 4: Initial Setup

You are almost ready to start sourcing! You just need to tell the AI where your resumes are located:

### A. Your Local Resume Folder
To allow the AI to search your personal database of candidate resumes:
1. Look for the **📁 Local ATS / Resumes** section on the left side of the dashboard.
2. Paste the exact path to the folder on your computer where you keep your candidate PDFs and Word documents (e.g., `C:\Users\John\Documents\Resumes`).
3. Click **Save Folder Path**.

### B. Email Sync Configuration (Optional)
If you want the dashboard to keep track of your active conversations:
1. In the **IMAP Config** section, enter your email address.
2. Enter your **App Password** (If you use Gmail, go to your Google Account Security settings to generate an "App Password").
3. Click **Save Credentials**.

---

## How to Use the Core Features

### 1. Adding a New Job (Live Roles)
- Fill out the "New Job Entry" form on the left side of the dashboard with the Job Title, Company, and Mandatory Skills. Click **Save the job**.

### 2. 📊 Req Analysis (AI Job Decoding)
- On any of your Active Jobs, click the **📊 Req Analysis** button. The AI will instantly generate complex, ready-to-use **Boolean Search Strings** that you can copy and paste into LinkedIn!

### 3. 📁 Scanning Local Resumes (AI Semantic Search)
- Click **📁 Scan Local Resumes** under the Admin Controls. The system will silently scan the folder you specified, read the text of every PDF/DOCX, and convert them into vectors so you can instantly search them based on meaning and context, not just keywords!

---
*Welcome to the future of recruitment! If the app ever needs an update, it will automatically download it in the background and gently prompt you to restart.*
