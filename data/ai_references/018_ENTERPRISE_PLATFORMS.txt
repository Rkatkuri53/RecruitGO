ENTERPRISE PLATFORM DEFINITION
==============================

Enterprise Platforms are large-scale business software systems that automate and integrate organizational processes.

Unlike traditional software development, enterprise platforms are primarily configured, customized, integrated, and extended to support business operations.

===============

Ignoring module specialization.
