PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Adobe Marketo Engage is Adobe's B2B marketing automation platform focused on demand generation, lead management, customer engagement and account-based marketing.

It enables organizations to automate marketing campaigns, score and nurture leads, integrate with CRM platforms and measure marketing contribution to pipeline and revenue.
