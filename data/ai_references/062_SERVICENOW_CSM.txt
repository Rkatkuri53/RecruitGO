PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

ServiceNow Customer Service Management (CSM) enables organizations to automate customer support operations through digital workflows, self-service portals, omnichannel support and proactive customer service.

It connects customers, support agents, field service teams and back-office operations through a unified platform.
