PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle Fusion Middleware (OFM) is Oracle's enterprise middleware platform that provides application servers, integration services, business process management, identity management and enterprise infrastructure.

Although Oracle Integration Cloud is Oracle's strategic cloud integration platform, Oracle Fusion Middleware remains widely deployed in enterprise on-premises and hybrid environments.
