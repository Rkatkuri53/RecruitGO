PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

IBM Cloud Pak is IBM's enterprise hybrid cloud platform portfolio built on Red Hat OpenShift. It enables organizations to modernize applications, deploy cloud-native workloads, automate business processes, integrate enterprise systems, manage AI workloads and operate across public cloud, private cloud and on-premises environments.

Cloud Paks provide containerized enterprise software with Kubernetes orchestration, DevSecOps pipelines, observability and lifecycle management.
