PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Microsoft Dynamics 365 is Microsoft's cloud-based suite of enterprise business applications that combines ERP, CRM, AI, analytics and low-code application development into a unified platform.

Dynamics 365 integrates natively with Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, Teams, Power Platform and Dataverse, enabling end-to-end digital transformation across finance, operations, sales, customer service, field service and marketing.
