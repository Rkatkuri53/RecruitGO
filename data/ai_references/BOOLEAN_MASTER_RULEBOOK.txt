Provide the master decision framework for all AI-powered Boolean generation, sourcing, candidate search, resume database search, X-Ray search, ATS search, and candidate matching.

This document is the highest-priority Boolean specification. Every recruitment AI agent must follow these rules before generating any Boolean search.

==================================================
BOOLEAN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

Boolean generation is NOT keyword matching.

Boolean generation is the process of translating business requirements into searchable candidate intent.

The AI must understand the role before generating any search.

Never search words.

Search concepts.
