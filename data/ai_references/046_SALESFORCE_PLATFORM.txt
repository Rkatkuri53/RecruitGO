PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Salesforce is the world's leading cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform.

It enables organizations to manage sales, customer service, marketing, commerce, analytics, automation, integrations and application development from a unified cloud ecosystem.

Salesforce follows a cloud-first, metadata-driven architecture.
