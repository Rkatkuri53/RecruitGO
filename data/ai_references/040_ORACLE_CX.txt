PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle CX Cloud (Customer Experience) is Oracle's cloud-based CRM platform that manages the complete customer lifecycle, from marketing and lead generation to sales, service, commerce, field service and customer retention.

Oracle CX integrates with Oracle ERP, Oracle SCM, Oracle HCM and third-party applications to provide a unified customer experience.
