PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Infor EAM is Infor's Enterprise Asset Management platform used to manage the complete lifecycle of physical assets across manufacturing, healthcare, public sector, utilities, transportation, facilities management and energy industries.

The platform supports preventive maintenance, predictive maintenance, inspections, inventory management, work management, reliability engineering and mobile field operations.
