PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Workday is a cloud-native enterprise platform providing Human Capital Management (HCM), Financial Management, Payroll, Planning and Analytics.

Unlike traditional ERP systems, Workday is delivered as Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) with biannual feature releases, centralized tenant management and configuration-driven implementation rather than heavy customization.
