PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Infor CloudSuite is Infor's industry-specific cloud ERP portfolio built primarily on Amazon Web Services (AWS). It delivers multi-tenant SaaS ERP solutions with embedded analytics, AI, workflow automation, integration and industry best practices.

CloudSuite offerings are tailored for manufacturing, distribution, healthcare, retail, hospitality, food & beverage, fashion and public sector organizations.
