COMMON_MISTAKES
===============

Confusing Procurement with Inventory Management.

Ignoring integration with FI, PP, SD, QM, and EWM.

Matching candidates solely because they mention Purchase Orders.

Treating SAP Ariba experience as equivalent to SAP MM implementation.

Ignoring business process ownership.

Assuming certification equals implementation experience.
