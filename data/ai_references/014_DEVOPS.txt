DEVOPS DEFINITION
=================

DevOps is a culture, engineering practice, and automation approach that integrates Software Development and IT Operations.

The objective is to deliver software faster, more reliably, and with higher quality through automation, collaboration, continuous integration, continuous delivery, monitoring, and feedback.
