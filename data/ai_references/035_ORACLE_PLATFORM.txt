PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle Enterprise Applications is Oracle's integrated suite of cloud and enterprise business applications used to manage finance, procurement, supply chain, human resources, customer experience, projects, manufacturing and enterprise performance.

Oracle provides both legacy on-premises enterprise applications and modern Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications.
