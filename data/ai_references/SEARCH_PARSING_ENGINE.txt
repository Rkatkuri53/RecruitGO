PURPOSE:
Define how an AI Recruiter parses a Job Description (JD) before generating Boolean search strings. This engine transforms unstructured hiring requirements into structured search intent. No Boolean should be generated until this parsing process is complete.

==================================================
SEARCH PARSING PHILOSOPHY
=========================

The AI MUST NOT generate a Boolean immediately after reading a Job Description.

Instead, it must first understand:

WHO is required?

WHY are they required?

WHAT will they do?

HOW will they do it?

WHERE will they work?

WHEN (experience level)?

Only after answering these questions may the AI generate Boolean.

==================================================
COMMON PARSING MISTAKES
=======================

Searching before understanding the JD.

Treating every mentioned skill as mandatory.

Ignoring industry context.

Ignoring implementation type.

Ignoring architecture responsibilities.

Ignoring leadership indicators.

Treating certifications as core skills.

Failing to distinguish core technologies from supporting tools.

==================================================
AI DECISION RULES
=================

Read the entire JD before extracting information.

Never infer mandatory skills without evidence.

Classify every extracted item into the correct category.

Identify business intent, not just keywords.

Generate structured search entities before Boolean.

If ambiguity exists, prefer evidence from responsibilities over job title.

Support hybrid roles (e.g., SAP + Finance, Java + DevOps).
