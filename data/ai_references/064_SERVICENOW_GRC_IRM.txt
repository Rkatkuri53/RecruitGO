PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

ServiceNow Integrated Risk Management (IRM), formerly Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC), enables organizations to identify, assess, monitor and mitigate enterprise risks while ensuring regulatory compliance and operational resilience.

It provides integrated workflows for policy management, audit, compliance, operational risk and third-party risk.
