PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle ERP Cloud (Oracle Fusion ERP) is Oracle's cloud-based Enterprise Resource Planning platform.

It integrates financial management, procurement, project management, risk management, expenses, and enterprise operations into a unified cloud solution.
