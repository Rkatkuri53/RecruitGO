PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Adobe Target is Adobe's enterprise personalization and experimentation platform used to optimize customer experiences through A/B testing, multivariate testing, AI-driven recommendations and real-time personalization.

Organizations use Adobe Target to improve digital conversion rates, customer engagement and business outcomes across websites, mobile applications and digital channels.
