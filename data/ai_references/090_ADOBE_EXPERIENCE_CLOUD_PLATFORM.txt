PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Adobe Experience Cloud is Adobe's enterprise Digital Experience Platform (DXP) for delivering personalized customer experiences across web, mobile, commerce, marketing and analytics.

The platform combines content management, customer data, personalization, campaign management, AI-driven recommendations and commerce into an integrated digital experience ecosystem.
