==================================================
MICROSERVICES DEFINITION
========================

Microservices Architecture is an architectural style where an application is divided into multiple independent services.

Each service is responsible for one business capability.

Each service can be developed, deployed, scaled and maintained independently.

COMMON_MISTAKES
===============

Treating Docker as Microservices.

Treating Kubernetes as Microservices.

Assuming REST automatically means Microservices.

Treating SOA and Microservices as identical.

Assuming every Spring Boot application is a Microservice.

Ignoring distributed system challenges such as resilience, observability and eventual consistency.

Matching candidates solely on Docker and Kubernetes keywords without architecture evidence.
