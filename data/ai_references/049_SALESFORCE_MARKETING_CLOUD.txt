=================

Salesforce Marketing Cloud is Salesforce's enterprise digital marketing platform used to automate customer engagement across email, SMS, mobile, social media, advertising and personalized customer journeys.

It enables organizations to deliver omnichannel marketing campaigns driven by customer behavior and real-time data.
