PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Adobe Experience Platform (AEP) is Adobe's enterprise customer data platform that unifies customer data from multiple sources into real-time customer profiles for analytics, personalization and customer journey orchestration.

AEP serves as the data foundation for multiple Adobe Experience Cloud products including Adobe Target, Adobe Campaign, Customer Journey Analytics and Journey Optimizer.
