PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Workday Extend enables organizations to build secure custom applications that run natively on the Workday platform.

Rather than modifying core Workday functionality, Extend applications use Workday APIs, platform services and business objects to deliver custom workflows while preserving upgrade compatibility.
