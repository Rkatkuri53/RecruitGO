PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

ServiceNow Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM), formerly Project Portfolio Management (PPM), enables organizations to manage enterprise demand, projects, portfolios, investments, agile delivery and strategic initiatives from a single platform.

It helps executives align business strategy with technology investments and delivery execution.
