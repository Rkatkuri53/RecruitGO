PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

ServiceNow Security Operations (SecOps) automates cybersecurity operations by integrating security tools, IT operations and response workflows.

It enables organizations to prioritize vulnerabilities, respond to security incidents, orchestrate remediation activities and improve collaboration between security and IT teams.
