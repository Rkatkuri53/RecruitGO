PURPOSE:
Define how AI identifies, classifies, prioritizes, weights, and expands skills extracted from Job Descriptions (JDs) before Boolean generation and candidate matching.

This engine determines which skills are mandatory, optional, supporting, transferable, or irrelevant, ensuring Boolean searches target the correct candidate population.

==================================================
SKILL CLASSIFICATION PHILOSOPHY
===============================

Not all skills have equal importance.

The AI must never assume every mentioned technology or tool is mandatory.

Every extracted skill must be classified before being used in Boolean generation.

==================================================
COMMON PARSING ERRORS
=====================

Treating every listed tool as mandatory.

Confusing supporting tools with core technologies.

Treating certifications as skills.

Ignoring business domain expertise.

Ignoring transferable skills.

Failing to distinguish platform from module.

==================================================
AI DECISION RULES
=================

Always identify the minimum skill set required to perform the role.

Separate mandatory from optional skills.

Expand every critical technology with known synonyms.

Normalize abbreviations before Boolean generation.

Use transferable skills only to broaden searches, never to replace explicit requirements.

Avoid overloading the Boolean with low-value tools.
