==================================================
PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Infor LN is Infor's enterprise ERP solution for discrete manufacturing organizations, particularly companies operating engineer-to-order (ETO), make-to-order (MTO), configure-to-order (CTO) and project-based manufacturing models.

Originally known as BAAN ERP, Infor LN is widely implemented across aerospace & defense, automotive, industrial manufacturing, electronics and high-tech industries.
