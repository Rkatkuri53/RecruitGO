PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Infor M3 is Infor's enterprise ERP platform designed primarily for process manufacturing, distribution and equipment-intensive industries.

Originally known as Movex, Infor M3 supports manufacturing, finance, procurement, supply chain, sales, warehouse management and industry-specific business processes for food & beverage, fashion, chemicals, distribution and equipment manufacturing.
