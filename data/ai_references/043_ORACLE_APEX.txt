PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle Application Express (APEX) is Oracle's low-code development platform for rapidly building secure, scalable, database-driven web applications.

Oracle APEX is tightly integrated with Oracle Database and is widely used for internal enterprise applications, dashboards, workflow solutions and business portals.
