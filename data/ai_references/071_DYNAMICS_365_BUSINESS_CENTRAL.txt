PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Dynamics 365 Business Central is Microsoft's cloud ERP solution for small and mid-sized businesses.

It evolved from Microsoft Dynamics NAV and provides integrated finance, purchasing, inventory, sales, warehousing, manufacturing, service management and project management capabilities.
