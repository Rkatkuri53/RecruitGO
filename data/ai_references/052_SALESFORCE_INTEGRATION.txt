PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Salesforce Integration enables Salesforce to securely exchange data with enterprise systems, cloud applications, databases, ERPs, HR platforms and third-party services.

Modern Salesforce integrations use APIs, events and middleware rather than direct point-to-point connections.

Ignoring authentication and security architecture.
