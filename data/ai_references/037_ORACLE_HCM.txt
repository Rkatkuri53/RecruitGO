PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle HCM Cloud (Oracle Fusion HCM) is Oracle's cloud-based Human Capital Management platform.

It manages the complete employee lifecycle including recruitment, onboarding, workforce management, payroll, talent management, compensation, learning, succession planning and HR analytics.
