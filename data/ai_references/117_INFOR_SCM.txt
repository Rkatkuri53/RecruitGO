PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Infor Supply Chain Management (SCM) is Infor's suite of supply chain applications supporting demand planning, supply planning, inventory optimization, procurement, transportation, logistics and end-to-end supply chain collaboration.

The platform enables organizations to optimize inventory levels, improve forecast accuracy, streamline logistics and increase supply chain visibility across manufacturing and distribution networks.
