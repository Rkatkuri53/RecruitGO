PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

ServiceNow is a cloud-based enterprise workflow platform that digitizes and automates business processes across IT, HR, Customer Service, Security, Risk, Asset Management and Operations.

Originally focused on IT Service Management (ITSM), ServiceNow has evolved into a comprehensive enterprise workflow platform used by organizations worldwide.
