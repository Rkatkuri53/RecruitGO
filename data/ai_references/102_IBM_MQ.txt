PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

IBM MQ is IBM's enterprise messaging middleware that enables reliable, asynchronous communication between distributed applications.

It provides guaranteed message delivery, transactional messaging, high availability and secure communication across heterogeneous enterprise environments.
