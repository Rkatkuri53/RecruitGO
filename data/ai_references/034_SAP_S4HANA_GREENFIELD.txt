OVERVIEW
========

A Greenfield implementation is a completely new SAP S/4HANA implementation.

Business processes are designed from the beginning using SAP Best Practices and Fit-to-Standard methodology.

Greenfield projects emphasize process standardization, Clean Core, business transformation and cloud readiness.

COMMON_MISTAKES
===============

Treating Greenfield and Brownfield as interchangeable.

Matching candidates solely because they mention SAP S/4HANA.

Confusing Rollout projects with Greenfield implementations.

Ignoring Fit-to-Standard workshop experience.

Ignoring SAP Activate methodology.

Treating support projects as implementation projects.

Assuming certification equals Greenfield implementation experience.
