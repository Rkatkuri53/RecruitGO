PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

ServiceNow Integration enables enterprise workflow automation by securely connecting the Now Platform with cloud applications, on-premises systems, identity providers, monitoring tools, HR systems, ERPs and collaboration platforms.

Modern ServiceNow integrations rely on APIs, IntegrationHub spokes, Flow Designer and MID Server rather than custom point-to-point scripting.
