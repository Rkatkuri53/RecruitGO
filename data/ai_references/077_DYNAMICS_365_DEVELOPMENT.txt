=================

Dynamics 365 development extends Microsoft business applications using Microsoft's development stack.

Developers customize CRM and ERP applications through plugins, extensions, client scripting, APIs, Power Platform, X++ (Finance & Operations), AL (Business Central), and Azure services while following Microsoft's Application Lifecycle Management (ALM) practices.
