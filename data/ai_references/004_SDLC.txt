SDLC OVERVIEW
=============

Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is the structured process used to plan, build, test, deploy, operate and continuously improve software systems.

Every IT role participates in one or more SDLC phases.

Understanding SDLC participation is essential for accurate JD parsing, Boolean generation and candidate matching.

==================================================
AI_DECISION_RULES
=================

Identify every SDLC phase mentioned in the JD.

Identify every SDLC phase demonstrated in the resume.

Compare responsibilities phase by phase.

Do not assume development experience automatically includes design or deployment.

Use SDLC participation to infer seniority.

Senior engineers usually participate in:

Design

Code Reviews

Mentoring

Production Support

Architects usually participate in:

Requirements

Architecture

Technology Selection

Governance

Project Managers usually participate in:

Planning

Risk

Stakeholder Management

Delivery

==================================================
COMMON_MISTAKES
===============

Assuming every developer performs architecture.

Assuming every QA engineer performs automation.

Treating production support as development.

Ignoring deployment responsibilities.

Ignoring maintenance and enhancement work.

Matching only programming languages while ignoring SDLC responsibilities.
