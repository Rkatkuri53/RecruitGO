PURPOSE:
Provide the foundational principles of Boolean search for AI agents generating recruiter-grade search strings across ATS databases, resume databases, LinkedIn, Google X-Ray, GitHub, Stack Overflow, job boards, and enterprise sourcing platforms.

This document defines Boolean logic, search behavior, grouping principles, operator usage, recruiter best practices, and universal search standards.

==================================================
BOOLEAN DEFINITION
==================

Boolean Search is the process of combining logical operators and search expressions to retrieve the most relevant records while minimizing irrelevant results.

Recruitment Boolean is NOT keyword search.

Recruitment Boolean is Intent Search.

The objective is to discover qualified candidates, not documents.
