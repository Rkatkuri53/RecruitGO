PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Dynamics 365 Marketing, now branded as Customer Insights - Journeys, is Microsoft's enterprise marketing automation platform.

It enables organizations to orchestrate real-time customer journeys, automate campaigns, nurture leads, personalize communications and measure marketing effectiveness across multiple channels.
