PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Salesforce CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote), now part of Revenue Cloud, automates the entire Quote-to-Cash (Q2C) lifecycle.

It enables organizations to configure products, calculate pricing, generate quotes, manage approvals, subscriptions, renewals and revenue operations.
