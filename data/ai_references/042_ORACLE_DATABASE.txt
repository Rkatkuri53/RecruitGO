PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle Database is an enterprise-grade relational database management system used for high-performance OLTP, analytics, mission-critical applications and enterprise workloads.

Oracle DBAs are responsible for installation, configuration, security, performance, backup, recovery, replication, upgrades and database lifecycle management.
