PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) Cloud helps organizations manage budgeting, forecasting, financial close, consolidation, profitability, tax reporting and strategic planning.

Oracle EPM Cloud is the successor to many traditional Oracle Hyperion solutions and supports enterprise financial transformation.
