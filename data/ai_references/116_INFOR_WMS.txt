PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Infor WMS is Infor's enterprise warehouse management platform designed to optimize warehouse operations, inventory visibility, labor productivity and distribution processes.

The platform supports advanced receiving, put-away, replenishment, picking, packing, shipping, cross-docking, wave management, automation integration and real-time warehouse execution.
