PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Dynamics 365 Finance & Supply Chain Management is Microsoft's enterprise ERP solution for medium and large organizations.

It manages finance, accounting, procurement, inventory, warehouse operations, manufacturing, planning, logistics and enterprise operations through an integrated cloud platform.

Formerly known as Dynamics AX and Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (F&O).
