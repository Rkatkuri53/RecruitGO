PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

IBM FileNet is IBM's enterprise content management (ECM) platform used to manage digital documents, records, business content, workflows and case management across highly regulated industries.

It enables organizations to securely capture, store, govern, retrieve and automate enterprise content throughout its lifecycle while supporting compliance and digital transformation initiatives.
