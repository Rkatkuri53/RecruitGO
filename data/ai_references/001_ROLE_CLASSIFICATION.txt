ROLE CLASSIFICATION RULES
=========================

Always classify a JD into:

Primary Role Family

Secondary Role Family (if applicable)

Specialization

Technology Stack

Business Domain

Project Type

Seniority

==================================================
MATCHING_RULES

Primary Role Match

Critical

Secondary Role Match

High

Specialization Match

High

Technology Match

Critical

Business Domain

Medium

==================================================
BOOLEAN_RULES

Generate Boolean using:

Primary Role

↓

Alternative Titles

↓

Specialization

↓

Core Technologies

Never generate Boolean from technologies alone.

==================================================
AI_DECISION_RULES

Every JD must have:

Exactly one Primary Role Family.

Zero or more Secondary Role Families.

One or more Specializations.

If multiple role families exist, classify as Hybrid Role.

==================================================
COMMON_MISTAKES

Using job title instead of actual responsibilities.

Confusing DevOps with Cloud.

Confusing SRE with DevOps.

Confusing Functional Consultants with Technical Consultants.

Confusing Solution Architects with Enterprise Architects.

Confusing Support roles with Development roles.
