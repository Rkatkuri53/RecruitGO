PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Adobe Campaign is Adobe's enterprise cross-channel campaign management platform used to design, automate and personalize customer communications across email, SMS, push notifications, direct mail and other digital channels.

It supports both batch and real-time marketing campaigns while integrating with Adobe Experience Platform and other Adobe Experience Cloud products.
