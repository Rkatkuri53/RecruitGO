==================================================
MODULE OVERVIEW
===============

SAP Basis is the technical foundation that installs, operates, monitors, secures and maintains SAP systems.

Basis consultants are responsible for system availability, performance, upgrades, transport management, patching, backup, recovery and technical administration.

They normally do NOT perform business process configuration.

COMMON_MISTAKES
===============

Treating SAP Basis as an SAP Functional role.

Confusing SAP Basis with ABAP Development.

Ignoring operating system and database administration.

Matching candidates solely because they list SAP.

Treating SAP HANA DBA experience as complete SAP Basis expertise.

Ignoring technical migration experience.

Assuming certification equals production administration experience.
