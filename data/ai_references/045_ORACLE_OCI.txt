PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) is Oracle's public cloud platform that provides Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS), Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS), containers, databases, networking, AI services, analytics and enterprise cloud capabilities.

OCI supports Oracle enterprise applications as well as cloud-native workloads.
