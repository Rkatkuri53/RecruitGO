PURPOSE:
Define how AI recognizes, normalizes, expands, and maps job titles before Boolean generation. Job titles vary significantly across companies, industries, and countries. This engine ensures equivalent titles are searched together while preventing unrelated roles from being merged.

==================================================
TITLE NORMALIZATION PHILOSOPHY
==============================

Job Titles are NOT standardized.

The same role can have dozens of different titles.

Never search a single title.

Always search

Role Concepts

NOT

Role Words.

COMMON TITLE MISTAKES
=====================

Searching only one title.

Ignoring regional title variations.

Ignoring abbreviations.

Mixing unrelated job families.

Ignoring seniority.

Ignoring technology specialization.

Treating company-specific titles as industry standards.

==================================================
AI DECISION RULES
=================

Normalize every extracted title.

Expand titles into equivalent market terminology.

Preserve technology specialization.

Preserve business function.

Separate title from seniority.

Separate title from industry.

Do not infer technology from generic titles alone.
