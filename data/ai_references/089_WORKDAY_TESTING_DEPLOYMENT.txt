PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Workday delivery emphasizes configuration validation, tenant lifecycle management, structured testing and controlled deployment rather than traditional software installation.

Organizations typically manage multiple tenants for implementation, testing, preview and production while adopting Workday's twice-yearly release cycle.
