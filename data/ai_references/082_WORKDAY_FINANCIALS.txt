PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Workday Financial Management is a cloud-native ERP solution providing unified financial management, accounting, procurement, expense management and reporting.

Unlike legacy ERP platforms, Workday Financials combines operational and financial data in a single cloud platform with real-time reporting and configurable business processes.
