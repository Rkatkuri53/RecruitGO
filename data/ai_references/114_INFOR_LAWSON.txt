PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Infor Lawson is Infor's enterprise ERP platform primarily serving healthcare, public sector, education and service organizations. It provides comprehensive financial management, human capital management (HCM), procurement, payroll and supply chain capabilities.

Modern Lawson implementations leverage Landmark technology, Infor FSM (Financials & Supply Management) and Infor HCM as part of the broader Infor CloudSuite ecosystem.
