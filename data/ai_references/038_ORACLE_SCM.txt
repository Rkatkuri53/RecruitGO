PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Oracle SCM Cloud (Oracle Fusion SCM) manages end-to-end supply chain operations from sourcing and procurement to manufacturing, inventory, logistics, warehousing, fulfillment and planning.

It provides integrated supply chain visibility and supports digital transformation across procurement, production and logistics operations.
