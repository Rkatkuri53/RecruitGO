MODULE OVERVIEW
===============

SAP Plant Maintenance (PM) manages the complete lifecycle of enterprise assets, equipment, maintenance operations, and maintenance planning.

It enables organizations to maximize equipment availability, reduce downtime, improve maintenance efficiency, and optimize asset lifecycle costs.
