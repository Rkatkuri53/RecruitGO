PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

IBM Sterling is IBM's enterprise supply chain and B2B integration platform providing order management, inventory visibility, supplier collaboration, managed file transfer, EDI integration and omnichannel fulfillment capabilities.

The platform enables organizations to optimize end-to-end supply chain operations, integrate trading partners and improve order lifecycle management across retail, manufacturing, logistics and distribution industries.
