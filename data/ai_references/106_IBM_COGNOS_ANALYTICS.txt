PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

IBM Cognos Analytics is IBM's enterprise business intelligence platform used for operational reporting, dashboards, self-service analytics, scorecards and executive decision support.

It enables organizations to build governed semantic models, enterprise reports, interactive dashboards and AI-assisted business insights from multiple enterprise data sources.
