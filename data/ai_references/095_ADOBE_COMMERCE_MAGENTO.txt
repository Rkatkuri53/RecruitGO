PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Adobe Commerce, formerly Magento Commerce, is Adobe's enterprise digital commerce platform supporting B2B and B2C commerce, omnichannel experiences, headless architecture and cloud-native deployments.

The platform provides catalog management, customer management, order processing, promotions, APIs and extensibility for enterprise commerce solutions.
