PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Workday Payroll provides an integrated payroll solution that combines workforce administration, compensation, time tracking, benefits and payroll processing within a single cloud platform.

Payroll configuration is closely integrated with Core HCM business processes, allowing organizations to automate payroll calculations while maintaining compliance with country-specific payroll regulations.
