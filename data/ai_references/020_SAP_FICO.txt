COMMON_MISTAKES
===============

Treating FI and CO as identical.

Assuming all FICO consultants have S/4HANA experience.

Matching on "SAP FICO" without validating actual configuration responsibilities.

Ignoring business process ownership.

Ignoring integration experience with MM, SD, PP, and HCM.

Treating certification as equivalent to implementation experience.
