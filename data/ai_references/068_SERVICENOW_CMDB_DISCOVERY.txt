PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

ServiceNow CMDB provides a centralized repository of Configuration Items (CIs) and their relationships across infrastructure, applications, cloud services and business services.

Discovery and Service Mapping automatically populate and maintain CMDB accuracy while CSDM provides standardized data modeling for enterprise service management.
