MODULE OVERVIEW
===============

SAP Extended Warehouse Management (EWM) manages complex warehouse operations from goods receipt to goods issue.

It supports inventory accuracy, warehouse automation, labor optimization, warehouse visibility, and high-volume logistics operations.
