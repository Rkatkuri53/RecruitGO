=================

Dynamics 365 Integration connects Microsoft business applications with enterprise systems, cloud services, on-premises platforms, and third-party applications using Azure Integration Services, Dataverse, APIs and event-driven architecture.

Modern Dynamics integrations prioritize scalable, loosely coupled and cloud-native integration patterns.
