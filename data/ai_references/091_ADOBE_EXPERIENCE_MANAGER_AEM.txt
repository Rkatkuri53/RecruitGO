PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) is Adobe's enterprise content management platform for websites, digital assets, forms and omnichannel content delivery.

Modern AEM implementations leverage AEM as a Cloud Service, headless content management, reusable content fragments and API-first architecture.
