OVERVIEW
========

A Brownfield implementation is an existing SAP ECC system converted to SAP S/4HANA while preserving most existing business processes, organizational structures, master data and transactional history.

Brownfield is NOT a new implementation.

It is a technical and business transformation.

==================================================
COMMON_MISTAKES
===============

Treating every S/4HANA project as Brownfield.

Confusing upgrades with system conversions.

Confusing Greenfield implementations with Brownfield transformations.

Matching candidates only because they mention SAP S/4HANA.

Ignoring technical conversion responsibilities.

Ignoring cutover leadership.

Ignoring evidence of production conversion.

Assuming certification equals Brownfield implementation experience.
