==================================================
AI_DECISION_RULES
=================

If responsibilities indicate a different role than the title

Normalize to the responsibility-based role.

If technologies indicate a different specialization

Normalize to the technology specialization.

If multiple technologies represent multiple role families

Classify as Hybrid Role.

==================================================
COMMON_MISTAKES
===============

Assuming "Software Engineer" always means Backend Developer.

Treating "Consultant" as a Functional Consultant without evidence.

Treating "Architect" as Enterprise Architect without architecture responsibilities.

Treating "Cloud Engineer" and "DevOps Engineer" as identical.

Treating "Platform Engineer" as DevOps without infrastructure responsibilities.

Treating "Support Engineer" as Software Developer.
