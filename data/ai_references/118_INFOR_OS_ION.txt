PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

Infor OS is Infor's enterprise digital platform that provides integration, workflow automation, API management, analytics, security and enterprise collaboration capabilities.

Infor ION (Intelligent Open Network) is the core integration framework within Infor OS, enabling application integration, document exchange, event-driven communication and business process orchestration across Infor and third-party applications.
