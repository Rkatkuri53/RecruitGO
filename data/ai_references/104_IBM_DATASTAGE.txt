PLATFORM OVERVIEW
=================

IBM DataStage is IBM's enterprise ETL and data integration platform used to extract, transform and load large volumes of structured and semi-structured data into enterprise data warehouses, lakes and analytics platforms.

It supports high-performance parallel processing, metadata-driven development and enterprise-scale batch data integration.
