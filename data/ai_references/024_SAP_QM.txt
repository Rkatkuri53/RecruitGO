MODULE OVERVIEW
===============

SAP Quality Management (QM) supports quality planning, quality inspections, defect management, regulatory compliance, supplier quality, production quality, and customer quality.

It enables organizations to ensure products meet defined quality standards throughout procurement, manufacturing, warehousing, and delivery.

COMMON_MISTAKES
===============

Treating SAP QM as Testing or QA Automation.

Confusing software QA with manufacturing Quality Management.

Ignoring integration with MM, PP, PM, SD and EWM.

Matching candidates solely because they mention inspections.

Treating ISO quality knowledge as SAP QM implementation experience.

Ignoring regulatory and compliance responsibilities.

Assuming certification equals implementation experience.
